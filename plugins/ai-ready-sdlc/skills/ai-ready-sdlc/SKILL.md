---
name: ai-ready-sdlc
description: Apply the organization AI-ready SDLC policy during repository work.
---

When working in a repository that adopts this package:

- treat the neutral policy files under `../../standards/` as the source of truth,
- look for the AI capability manifest,
- look for a test plan on high-risk changes,
- preserve observability patterns,
- do not treat AI output as sufficient for approval on its own.
